Top-Down Royale

Privacy policy and support. Last updated 26 November 2026.

What the game collects

Top-Down Royale has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location or any other personal information.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (level, XP, gems, unlocked items, settings and match statistics) is saved on your own device so the game can load it next time. Apart from what online play needs (below), it never leaves the device. Deleting the app, or clearing the site's data in a browser, removes it.

You can copy your progress out as a backup code from Options, and paste a code back in to restore it. The code is created and read on your device only.

Online play and friends

When you play online, the game connects to the game server and sends your controls, a made-up callsign (for example "SwiftOtter42"; you cannot type a name), the skin you are wearing, and a random number created on your device that identifies your copy of the game. If you use Friends, it also sends the friend codes on your list, so the server can tell you which of your friends are online. Friends see each other only when both have added the other's code.

The server keeps this in memory only while you are connected. It is not written to a database, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone. There is no chat.

Purchases

The game has no purchases with real money. XP and gems are earned by playing.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Support and contact

Questions, bug reports and feedback: open an issue at github.com/amarcuss/top-down-royale/issues.
